10 m

The giants pass at ten metres

Sperm whales live off our coast all year. From July to October, humpbacks come to calve. We swim with them quietly, on one breath, at a respectful distance.

20 m

The reef wall at twenty

Green and hawksbill turtles rest on the slope. At this depth you learn to equalise, relax your shoulders and let the water carry you.

30 m

Calm inside the shoal

Jacks circle in thousands. Your heart slows, your mind gets quiet. This is what training is for: feeling at home where most people feel rushed.

40 m

Forty metres of silence

Here the only light is your own. We'll get you here step by step, with a buddy and a safety line on every dive.
